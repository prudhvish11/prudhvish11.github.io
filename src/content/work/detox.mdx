A research project built with a 4-member team: detecting and moderating toxic comments on YouTube, published at an IEEE conference.

## The problem

YouTube comment sections generate more volume than any manual moderation process can keep up with. DeTox explored whether a lightweight, purpose-built classification pipeline could flag toxic comments for review at the point they're posted, rather than after the fact.

## How it works

Comments are pulled per-video through the YouTube Data API, cleaned in a preprocessing step, then passed through a classification model trained to separate toxic from non-toxic text. Flagged comments go to a moderator review step rather than being removed automatically — the system assists a decision, it doesn't make one unsupervised.

## Team and publication

This was a 4-member college research project, not solo work. It was published as:
