An earlier attempt to move NLP work out of notebooks and into something people could actually use in a browser. Five language tools, one deployed application, no separate scripts to run.

## What it does

- **Text to speech** — converts written input to spoken audio.
- **Speech to text** — transcribes spoken audio to text.
- **Spell Corrector** — flags and corrects misspelled input.
- **Spam Classifier** — labels text as spam or not.
- **Sentiment Analyzer** — reads the sentiment of a piece of text.

## Why one interface

Each of these started as a separate notebook experiment. Packaging them behind one Flask front end turned five disconnected scripts into a single, still-running product — the shift from "code that works on my machine" to "a link anyone can open."
