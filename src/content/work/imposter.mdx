A social deduction game — the "who's the impostor" format, built for a real friend group instead of staying a nice idea. Working title Imposter along the way; shipped as **Vidu Kadhu**.

## Game flow

Create a room, share the code, and everyone joins from their own phone. One player gets a different word from everyone else — the game is figuring out who, before they figure out how to blend in.

## Product direction, not just code

The room flow, the reveal moment, the pacing of a round — those are product and UX decisions, made the same way any social game's design gets made. AI-assisted development turned the direction into a working build; the direction itself didn't come from a prompt.

## Playful, on purpose

Where LifeOS is calm and systemic, this is meant to feel energetic and social — brighter colors, a friendlier mascot, less chrome. Different product, same personal-lab process: concept, iteration, ship.
